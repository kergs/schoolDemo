About Enzy Royal

Learning, discipline and joy belong together. Our teachers know every child by name, and parents stay close to their child’s progress all term.

Our mission

To give every child a sound education and the character to use it well.

Our values

Integrity, hard work, respect for others and service to the community.

Our promise

Caring teachers, clear discipline and honest reports to parents.

Academics

A clear path from the first day of nursery to the final exam.

Nursery

Play-based learning, phonics and early numbers.

Primary

Core subjects, ICT and creative arts.

Junior secondary

A broad curriculum and Basic Education Certificate preparation.

Senior secondary

Science, Arts and Commercial tracks, with WAEC and NECO preparation.
